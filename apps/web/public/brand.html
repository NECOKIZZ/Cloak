Logo

The mark is a hood seen from the side: something covered, not hidden away. Use the wordmark where there's room, the mark alone where there isn't. Keep clear space equal to the height of the mark on every side, and never stretch, rotate, outline or recolour it outside the three versions here.

Colour

Four colours do all the work. Ink and paper carry almost everything; Cloak teal means sealed, private and live; Seal amber marks the public side, the clearing price and warnings. Dark mode uses the paired values. Click a value to copy it.

The fractal glass

Our signature texture: ribbed glass in teal over ink, like looking at something through a privacy screen. Use it as a background behind headlines and the mark, darkened where text sits. On light designs it appears inverted and faint. Never put it behind long text or in the product UI.

Voice

Calm, plain and exact. We say what a thing does and what it means for the person, in short sentences. No hype, no jargon, no promises we can't show on chain.

Do

  • Lead with the result: "Everyone pays one fair price."
  • Use plain words a first-time user knows.
  • Say what stays public, too.
  • Keep posts to one idea.

Don't

  • Use em dashes, colons or semicolons in posts.
  • Say "anonymous", "untraceable" or "100% private".
  • Use price talk, "moon", or financial advice.
  • Explain the cryptography unless asked.
