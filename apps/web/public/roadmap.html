Cloak

Roadmap

Privacy for Elysium. Fair launches nobody can snipe, and trading nobody can trace.

Live on testnet

What Cloak does today

  • Sealed-bid token launches. Everyone bids privately, everyone pays one fair price, and liquidity is locked from day one.
  • Private balances. Shield HYPE, USDC and launch tokens, then swap, send and withdraw without linking your wallet.
  • Batch trading. Orders are grouped and settled at one price, so single trades can't be front-run or traced.
  • Payment links. Send money as a link, split it into shares, or request a payment.
  • Sealed NFT drops. Mint without gas wars, auction NFTs you already own, and sell whitelist spots fairly.
  • Uniswap v4 launches with creator fees and anti-snipe protection.
  • For builders. A TypeScript SDK and an MCP server so AI agents can use Cloak.
  • .ely names. Withdraw to a name instead of an address, and see names across the app.
Next

Mainnet

  • Public trusted-setup ceremony with outside contributors.
  • External security audit.
  • Multisig ownership, with a public 3-day delay on any rule change.
  • Mainnet deployment, with every contract source-verified.
  • Deposit caps stay on until the audit and the ceremony are done.
After mainnet

Growing the private layer

  • Partner launchpads. A shared private vault for tokens from other Elysium launchpads, so their users get the same privacy.
  • Creator dashboard and launch pages that keep working after a launch ends.
  • Private holder proofs. Prove you hold a token without revealing your wallet.
  • Notifications for bids, fills and claims.
  • Run-your-own keeper and relayer, so anyone can operate the infrastructure.
  • More names everywhere, so payment links and agents show who sent and claimed.
